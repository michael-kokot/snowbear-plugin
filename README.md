# SnowBear

Find relevant companies and contacts, uncover warm connections, and coordinate outreach across LinkedIn and email with SnowBear. Draft personalized notes, send messages, engage relevant contacts, and manage follow-up in one workflow. Supports sales, hiring, and job seeking. SnowBear MCP tools and authenticated API endpoints provide research, drafting, campaign, contact, reply, approval and performance operations without automating the dashboard. read_docs and search_docs provide current reference for these operations. Account access requires authentication; sign-in and consent happen on SnowBear's website. UI features without a tool or API endpoint require separate browser interaction.

SnowBear helps you identify and engage professional contacts through LinkedIn and email. Connect your SnowBear account through OAuth, then ask for relevant companies and contacts, warm connections, personalized notes, coordinated outreach or campaign results. The companion skill explains workflows and consequential changes. Real outreach remains subject to your account permissions, approvals, schedules and allowances. See https://www.snowbear.ai/privacy and https://www.snowbear.ai/terms. Sign in or create your SnowBear account on the authorization page.
