## Adding people to a campaign
A campaign gets people two ways, and they run side by side.
- Find new contacts through the campaign's selected targeting (ideal_persona, ideal_company, target_functions) or its user-selected LinkedIn or Sales Navigator people search (sourcing.method linkedin_search_url). These are campaign-sourced records, separate from contacts added through lists. The configured sequence, approval settings and account limits determine whether a sourced contact can enter outreach. Explicit sequence_id:null keeps discovery only, without drafts or enrollments.
  - Use PATCH /campaigns/{id} only for requested targeting or search-source changes, such as "target VPs of Sales instead of account executives" or "use this selected Sales Navigator search". If the user wants more leads matching current targeting, retain those criteria, read sourcing progress and reuse available saved results. Explain the current cadence or blocker rather than changing configuration just to obtain more results. A request for more leads does not by itself authorize raising new_people_per_day, increasing sending volume, changing approval settings or activating outreach. Change a daily target only when the request covers that setting.
  - When I hand you a LinkedIn people-search link, that is this way, not a list: people in the search are sourced over the following mornings; they are contacted only when outreach is enabled and its approvals and sending rules allow it.
  - Don't look people up yourself and add them one by one to get more leads: SnowBear's sourcing workflow already saves the selected results and skips people already contacted. AI-search targeting and a supplied people-search link have different fit-review behavior; do not claim a search-link audience received an additional AI fit check.
- Add contacts (I bring them, through lists). People I name, upload or already know go on a list the campaign draws from. When list outreach is enabled, SnowBear reaches up to existing_contacts.per_day of them a day per sender, separately from new_people_per_day, in turn and within the campaign's review and approval rules.
  - Use this when I give you specific people: names, LinkedIn URLs, emails, a spreadsheet, a CRM export, a conference attendee list.
  - There are two ways to add them. POST /campaigns/{id}/contacts: with list_id they go on that list; without it, on the campaign's own "<campaign name> - general known contacts" list, created the first time. Either way the list is added to the campaign if it isn't on it yet. Or POST /lists/{list_id}/people on a list the campaign already uses, when they belong with an existing group such as a conference list or a CRM segment.
  - Before adding, ask me which list unless I already said: one of the campaign's lists (existing_contacts.lists on GET /campaigns/{id}), another of my lists (GET /lists), a new list (POST /lists, then POST /campaigns/{id}/contacts with its list_id, which adds the list to the campaign), or the campaign's general list. If I don't mind, use the general list.
  - People you add aren't contacted right away. Each weekday morning SnowBear picks people from the campaign's lists, ranked against the campaign, up to its daily limit. When list outreach is enabled, it also starts a run as soon as you add them, so if today's limit has room some start today. On a paused campaign they wait until it's active.
  - Adding up to 25 people can take up to about a minute; retrying a request is safe (people already added come back already_on_list). Read the response's warnings and tell me about them: for example nothing can go out while the senders' LinkedIn needs reconnecting.
  - People you add join the campaign right away, even while it's paused, so you can prioritize or unenroll them before their outreach starts. (Someone added to a list with filters joins when the campaign's next run checks them against the filters.)
  - GET /campaigns/{id}/contacts shows where each one stands: while outreach.status is not_started, outreach.pending {code, label, detail} says why, with the same words the dashboard shows (Pending, Queued for Today, Awaiting Review, Drafting, Draft Failed, Not a Fit, In Other Outreach, Current Client, No Eligible Sender, Needs LinkedIn).
  - If I want someone reached sooner, use POST /campaigns/{id}/contacts/prioritize, and only when I ask.
- How the two combine: the campaign's daily outreach is the two added together. Each list can have its own review, sequence and company discovery settings (existing_contacts.lists); people SnowBear finds use the campaign's own. In GET /campaigns/{id}/contacts, each row's source says how they came: {kind: "found_by_snowbear"}, {kind: "list", list_id, list_name}, {kind: "moved"} (moved in from another campaign) or {kind: "added"} (added directly before lists).
- When it's unclear which I mean (for example "add more people to this campaign"), ask: should SnowBear find more people like the ones it targets, or do I have specific people to add?
