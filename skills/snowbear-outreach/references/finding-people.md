### Finding and enriching people
When you find people, companies or jobs for me (here or on your own), the usual path is: search, enrich the people worth keeping, add them to a campaign for approval, then review and approve what SnowBear drafts. Enriching first gives SnowBear's drafts real, sourced facts to work with. If I only want more leads for a campaign, don't search here: SnowBear finds them itself every weekday (read [Adding people to a campaign](adding-people.md)), and these searches are for people or companies I want to pick myself.
- GET /search/companies?q=...: describe the companies the way their own homepage would: what they sell or do and who they serve, 6 to 14 words, e.g. "companies selling payroll software to mid-market businesses". Never describe the need or pain my product solves (that returns vendors of it), never write a negation, and run 2 or 3 searches for different kinds of buyer. Start with my home metro for at most two searches, then widen, passing what you already have as exclude_domains. Then judge each result's fit yourself: not my own company or a competitor, and its core business must match, not a side offering.
- GET /search/people?company=...&domain=...&roles=...: pass the company's domain whenever you know it, so people at a same-name company are left out. One role family per search; for a person's location use a country rather than a city I'm only starting with. The role you searched for is only how someone was found, never proof of their job: read current_title. Similar names, agencies, clients and subsidiaries aren't a match without evidence; when it matters, enrich them.
- GET /search/jobs?q=...: one role family, the kind of employer in your own words, and one location per search (12 to 25 words works best; two locations means two searches). No employee counts, dates, excluded employers or my biography in the query. It starts with employers' own job boards from the last 30 days; if results are thin or repetitive, search again with ats_only=false.
- GET /jobs/verify?url=...&company=...&title=...: before you tell me a job is open, check it. status is open, closed or unverified. Use posting (title, company, location, salary) from the result rather than what the search page said, and link to employer_posting_url when there is one. unverified means SnowBear couldn't confirm it (often a job board that only copies postings), not that it's closed.
- POST /contacts/enrich: {"people": [...], "include": [...]} saves and enriches up to 10 people. Prefer linkedin_url; an email or a name plus company is matched for you. needs_confirmation means SnowBear isn't sure which profile it is: show me the candidates, and resend with the linkedin_url I pick. Never guess. When SnowBear took a match itself (matched), say which profile it picked (name and headline) so I can catch a wrong one. Ask before including work_email, personal_email or phone: each one found uses credits.
- GET /contacts/{contact_id}/enrichment: check every 30 seconds until done is true. research facts each have a source; use only what a fact says, with its date in mind, and never present a fact as recent when its date is old. Then add the person to a campaign with POST /campaigns/{id}/contacts so SnowBear drafts their outreach.
